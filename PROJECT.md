# Ridgelines: project state

TRMNL recipe: one real mountain range per day, drawn as a top-down map of gently rippled lines.

**Status (2026-10-02):** beta set up. 30 ranges in Germany and Austria, built in GitHub Actions and served from GitHub Pages; recipe files in recipe/. Not yet confirmed on a device. The worldwide build follows after the What to Wear overhaul.

Full requirements live in [docs/brief.md](docs/brief.md), a snapshot of the living brief exported on 2026-10-01. Where this file and the brief disagree, this file is newer.

## Architecture

Beta (built 2026-10-02): `pipeline/entries.py` builds each listed range from Copernicus GLO-90 and Wikidata; `pipeline/publish.py` writes one payload per calendar slot to `site/beta/d/<n>.json` and the Polling URL; `.github/workflows/site.yml` deploys `site/` to GitHub Pages; the Polling URL picks today's slot from TRMNL's clock plus the user's UTC offset, as Downstream does (its D18). No server. The planned full architecture below still applies to the worldwide list.

### Planned for the full list

1. **Build pipeline** (GitHub Actions, run on demand): Wikidata list of ranges, Copernicus GLO-90 crop windows over HTTP range requests, resample to a metric grid, spike cleanup, 8-bit base64 heightmap plus a per-entry height floor, one JSON file per entry plus a calendar index, uploaded to R2.
2. **Cloudflare Worker:** maps the user's local date (TRMNL sends the UTC offset in seconds) and Area setting to an entry and returns its file. No KV, no writes, long edge caching.
3. **TRMNL template:** plugin JS decodes the heightmap and draws SVG lines with framework paint colours and px() scaling. Caption sits in the native title bar.

## Findings

Each finding is dated. Re-check before relying on it.

- **2026-10-01, framework version.** https://trmnl.com/framework/docs/responsive redirects to 3.4, and the docs version picker lists 3.4, 3.1, 3.0, 2.3, 1.2 (fetched 2026-10-01). `https://trmnl.com/css/latest/plugins.css` and `/js/latest/plugins.js` serve the framework that tools/mockup renders with. *Re-check:* open https://trmnl.com/framework and stamp the current version here.
- **2026-10-01, framework renders.** tools/mockup now renders real Copernicus entries through the framework's own CSS, JS and fonts in Chromium (render.py, sweep.py). Pages need `<body class="environment trmnl">`, or no framework rule applies. The framework scales the screen by `--pixel-ratio` itself, so TRMNL X captures at 1872 x 1404 physical pixels. *Re-check:* delete tools/mockup/.cache/fw and re-render after any framework release.
- **2026-10-01, drawing container.** `layout layout--col layout--stretch` holding a `w--full h--full` box gives the drawing its full size (OG full 780 x 411, quadrant 365 x 173). `layout--stretch` with a `stretch-y` box collapses to 0 width, and the zero guard in template/ridgelines.js then draws nothing. The sweep drew all 144 combinations of 8 entries x 18 views and devices. *Re-check:* `python sweep.py views` and confirm `views_report.json` has no `"drawn": false`.
- **2026-10-01, line counts at gap px(6.1).** At ripple 5, the sweep measured: OG full 63, OG portrait 115, half horizontal 24, half vertical 62, quadrant 24. TRMNL X full 112, X portrait 155, X half horizontal and quadrant 48. Kindle 2024 full 64. `TRMNLPaint.px()` returned 6.1 on every device, so spacing holds in CSS pixels as the brief intends. Half horizontal and quadrant fall short of the brief's "about 30" because their layouts are 173 px tall. *Decided 2026-10-01 (Niko):* the counts are fine (29 with the title bar off).
- **2026-10-01, ripple scale.** Scaling ripples to the top of the stored square flattened Karwendel badly, because the 1.4x square reaches higher Stubai and Tux summits (up to 3,146 m) outside the range. Scaling to an entry `top` (the highest sample in the OG full window) restores the chosen look in every view. See docs/mockups/compare_resolution.png. *Re-check:* any entry whose neighbours outside the reference window are higher than its own peak.
- **2026-10-01, elevation spikes on GLO-90.** Capping at the documented high point is wrong. All 1,110 Karwendel samples above 2,769 m are real neighbouring peaks outside the range proper (29 clusters, all in the Stubai and Tux Alps at the square's southern edge). fixtures.py now treats a sample as a spike only if it is 250 m above its 7 x 7 median: 1 hit in Karwendel, 10 in Lyngen (sea cliffs, unchecked), 0 elsewhere. *Re-check:* look at the 10 Lyngen hits before the build adopts the rule.
- **2026-10-01, heightmap size.** A 200 x 200 grid (54 kB entry JSON, all 8 measured) is enough. On the OG it is visually indistinguishable from 280 (105 kB) and 400 (214 kB), and TRMNL X full still reads smooth. *Re-check:* on a real TRMNL X panel.
- **2026-10-01, floor and ripple across the corpus.** The 40th percentile floor with 4 gap ripples suits Karwendel, but turns Glockner, Teton and Lyngen into busy texture on 1-bit. The 55th percentile with 5 gaps reads best across all eight (docs/mockups/corpus_tuned_og.png). The 70th percentile suits Teton and Glockner but strips Cuillin and Rwenzori. *Decided 2026-10-01:* default floor 55th percentile, ripple 5 gaps, still tunable per entry.
- **2026-10-01, broad massifs.** Rwenzori draws as a soft swell at any floor, because the massif's dome takes most of the height range. Subtracting 70 % of a 4 km blur (local relief) gives it ridges and helps Glockner too, with little change elsewhere (docs/mockups/compare_relief.png). *Decided 2026-10-01:* local relief for every entry. Trade-off: ripple height shows how sharp the terrain is more than how high; the info box still gives the true summit height.
- **2026-10-01, Wikidata high points.** Uluigalau (Q5856020, Taveuni) is listed at 179.967 E. That point is sea (0 m in GLO-90, 279 m maximum within 2 km), while 179.967 W reads 674 m with 1,109 m within 2 km, so the sign is wrong. Wikidata lists Mount Liebig (1,524 m) as the MacDonnell high point, though Mount Zeil (1,531 m) is widely cited as higher. GROUP_CONCAT returns countries in no stable order, so fixtures.py sorts them. fixtures.py now snaps every high point to the highest terrain within 3 km of the listed point. If the listed point is below 75 % of the listed height, it also tries the sign-flipped longitude and latitude and keeps the summit that best matches the listed height. Before: the Taveuni label floated over the sea, west of the island. After: Taveuni takes the flipped longitude (summit 1,095 m in GLO-90, 2.8 km from the corrected point), and the label sits on the island's spine. The other seven keep their listed points and snap 0.1 to 1.2 km. *Re-check:* every entry's high point against terrain in the build, and Mount Zeil against a primary source.
- **2026-10-01, captions.** "Rwenzori Mountains · Mount Stanley 5,109 m · Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda" overflows the OG full title bar in English and German. It pushes into the plugin title and is cut at the right edge. OG portrait fits once the country is dropped (Karwendel). Quadrant and half views fit for all 8 in both languages. Missing German labels fall back to English (Teton Range, MacDonnell Ranges, Taveuni, Mount Uluigalau). *Open:* short country names, or drop the country when more than one.
- **2026-10-01, info box and title bar off (new defaults).** Without the title bar the drawing grows: OG full 71 lines, half horizontal and quadrant 29 (close to the brief's 30), half vertical 68, OG portrait 123, TRMNL X full 120. Across all 144 renders the map moved to keep the summit clear of the box 27 times. The label still touched the box once (Lyngen, TRMNL X half horizontal), because the stored square stopped the map moving far enough. The label now slides right of the box in that case (before: label under the box edge; after: label x 418, box right edge 412). The two-line clamp cut "Rwenzori Mountains, Democratic Republic o…" in the OG full box; with short country names it reads "Rwenzori Mountains, / DR Congo and Uganda" in two full lines (German "Ruwenzori-Gebirge, DR Kongo und Uganda" also fits). On OG 1-bit the "Highest peak" line in `description` renders very small. See docs/mockups/corpus_tuned_og.png. *Decided 2026-10-01:* `md:description--large` for the peak line in the full view. *Re-check:* `python sweep.py views` and the label/box overlap count from views_report.json.
- **2026-10-01, peak label at the edge.** When the summit sits near the top of a small view (MacDonnell quadrant and half horizontal), the label was cut off above the drawing. template/ridgelines.js now hangs it below the summit instead. *Re-check:* `python sweep.py views` and look at MacDonnell.
- **2026-10-02, GLO-90 licence.** The AWS registry links the Copernicus Data Space COP-DEM page, which links the licence bundle (copernicus_contributing_mission_data_access_v2_cop_dem_licenses.pdf, June 2025) and the ESA mission annex (CSCDA_ESA_Mission-specific_Annex_31_Oct_22_latest.pdf). Both contain the same separate licence, "COP-DEM-GLO-90-F Global 90m Full, Free & Open" (Article 6 compared word for word). It differs from the GLO-30 text the brief checked. Terms: rights to reproduce, distribute, communicate to the general public, and adapt, modify and combine; worldwide, no time limit, free of charge. No non-commercial clause, so Creator Fund payouts are fine. Obligations under Article 6: (a) the source notice for unmodified data. (b) For adapted data, which Ridgelines is (resampled, smoothed, local relief): "produced using Copernicus WorldDEM™-90 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved". (c) The liability sentence, translation allowed, "in a licence or any legal warning or notice covering" the distribution. (d) No implied endorsement. (e) Pass the obligations on to subsequent users. The licence names no place for the notice; it only says where the liability sentence goes. Using the WorldDEM trademark is allowed in this context. The Copernicus trademark has its own licence on copernicus.eu, not checked. The restricted GLO-30-R and EEA-10-R instances in the same bundle carry non-commercial clauses; they do not apply to GLO-90. fixtures.py now writes the exact notice, plus the liability sentence in English and German, into each entry. *Re-check:* reopen both PDFs from the COP-DEM page before launch, and read the Copernicus trademark licence if "Copernicus" appears outside the notice. Not legal advice.
- **2026-10-02, shading by height.** Tried on 2-bit OG and 4-bit TRMNL X for Karwendel, Glockner, Rwenzori and Cuillin, using framework grey tokens through TRMNLPaint.bg() (2-bit gets the framework's dither tile). The light version (gray-75 above half height) is barely visible on either screen. The two step version (gray-75 above 30 %, gray-55 above 65 %) marks the high ground, but leaves blocky rectangles where a fill runs past the next line, and competes with the lines. Look option `shade` in template/ridgelines.js (off by default, tuning only), sweep mode `shade`. *Decided 2026-10-02:* black lines only.
- **2026-10-08, worldwide candidates.** Live Wikidata query: distinct highest summits of mountain ranges (P610 with elevation and coordinates), by continent of the summit's country: Europe 1,199 (807 at 1,000 m or more), Asia 332 (300), North America 311 (294), Oceania 93 (68), Africa 57 (55), South America 50 (49), Eurasia 37 (32); summits on continental borders count twice. Africa: 2,979 ranges in Wikidata, 65 with a highest point and elevation, 2,842 with coordinates but no highest point, which the terrain fallback covers. So every area can reach 365, Africa and South America only with the fallback. *Re-check:* rerun both queries when building the list.
- **2026-10-02, Polling URL day flip.** `recipe/polling_url.txt` rendered with Ruby Liquid 5.14.0 (strict) for UTC offsets of minus 12, minus 10, minus 5, 0, plus 1, plus 2, plus 5:30, plus 9:30, plus 13 and plus 14 hours, each given as a number, a string and missing: 30 of 30 picked the slot of the local date (at 14:31 UTC, plus 9:30 already chose the next day's slot). The variable name `trmnl.user.utc_offset` comes from the Nearby Nextbike experience, not from the help article, which only says global variables such as the user's name and timestamp are available. *Re-check:* on a device, at local midnight, and by reading TRMNL's variables dropdown.
- **2026-10-02, templates render in Ruby Liquid.** All four views and the error screen render with Ruby Liquid in strict mode from the real payloads, and tools/mockup now renders every image through them. *Re-check:* `python tools/mockup/sweep.py views` after any template change.
- **2026-10-02, beta candidates in Germany and Austria.** Live Wikidata query: 382 mountain ranges (instances of Q46831 or its subclasses) with P17 Germany or Austria, plus a highest point (P610) that has elevation (P2044) and coordinates (P625). 2 lack a German label, 29 an English one. They share 297 distinct highest points, because nested groupings repeat summits (Alpen, Ostalpen, Zentrale Ostalpen and a SOIUSA duplicate all point to Mont Blanc or Piz Bernina; Hohe Tauern, Glocknergruppe and two more to the Großglockner). 278 of those summits lie in Germany or Austria themselves. By height: 267 at 300 m or more, 226 at 500 m, 157 at 800 m, 141 at 1,000 m. The lowest are hills such as the Rekener Berge (134 m). Curation for the beta: one entry per summit, drop groupings whose summit lies outside DE and AT, and drop hills too flat to draw (the build's flat flag decides, not a fixed height). *Re-check:* rerun the query when building the list; Wikidata changes.
- **2026-10-02, beta list built.** All 30 entries in data/beta_de_at.json build from GLO-90 and render on OG 1-bit at the defaults (docs/mockups/beta_1.png to beta_3.png). Each entry is about 55 kB. Every listed summit sits on high ground, so no sign flips were needed. Mangfallgebirge and Höllengebirge have 2 spike samples each, which the build replaces. Both open details are now settled: local peak names in every language, and "and 2 more" for the Rätikon (Decisions, 2026-10-02). *Re-check:* `python fixtures.py --list ../../data/beta_de_at.json`, then `python sweep.py beta`.
- **2026-10-01, payload cap.** Private plugins reject polled payloads over 100 KB (TRMNL help article "Parsing plugins with the Sandbox Runtime", dated Aug 28, 2026). Unknown whether the cap applies before or after compression. *Re-check:* reopen the article before sizing the payload.
- **2026-10-01, elevation spikes.** In the JAXA demo tiles, raw pixels in the Karwendel crop reach 3,011 m (2,935 m at the point sampled for the brief). The documented high point is 2,749 m (Birkkarspitze). tools/mockup clips to the documented maximum and patches around the spike. *Re-check:* run the fixture corpus through Copernicus GLO-90 and count pixels above each range's documented high point.
- **2026-10-01, busy terrain.** Without a height floor the drawing is too busy on 1-bit screens. A floor at the 40th percentile calmed the chosen mockup. *Re-check:* across the full fixture corpus.
- **2026-10-01, portrait caption.** At 480 px wide, range, peak, height and country do not fit beside the plugin title. *Open:* drop the country in portrait, or drop the title. Framework render 2026-10-01: with the country dropped, Karwendel fits beside the title on OG portrait.
- **2026-10-02, uniqueness.** The store's own search (trmnl.com/recipes?search=) gave: ridgeline 0, terrain 7, elevation 9, topo 12, contour 3 recipes plus 1 group, mountain 8 recipes plus 1 group. The search is fuzzy: terrain matches train boards, elevation matches "station", topo matches "top", contour matches "contest". Landscape hits: Watchtower (120 connections) draws generated hills; Shan Shui (1,080) draws invented ink mountains; Magic Hour (54) shows sunrise skies over mountains; plus snow and avalanche reports. None draws real terrain from elevation data. *Re-check:* repeat the six searches before submitting the recipe.
- **2026-10-01, mockup reproduction.** `tools/mockup/mockup.py` with default settings reproduces the chosen mockup exactly (0 of 384,000 pixels differ). *Re-check:* after any change to the tool, diff against docs/mockups/chosen_topdown_64_lines.png.
